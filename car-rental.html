Sightseeing Guide

Places To Visit In Kolkata with Our Cabs

Explore the City of Joy in comfort. Our chauffeurs know every heritage route and traffic shortcut.

1) Victoria Memorial

The white marble masterpiece dedicated to Queen Victoria, surrounded by lush gardens, galleries, and evening sound & light shows.

2) Howrah Bridge (Rabindra Setu)

The iconic cantilever bridge over the Hooghly river connecting Howrah and Kolkata, one of the busiest architectural marvels on earth.

3) Dakshineshwar Kali Temple

Revered Hindu temple complex built by Rani Rashmoni along the Hooghly river, sanctified by mystic saint Sri Ramakrishna Paramahamsa.

4) Indian Museum

Founded in 1814, it is the ninth oldest regular museum of the world with rare collections of antiques, fossils, mummies, and Mughal art.

5) Kalighat Kali Temple

One of the 51 Shakti Peethas in Hindu tradition, drawing pilgrims from across the globe to receive Maa Kali's blessings.

6) Science City

The largest science centre in the Indian subcontinent, featuring the Space Odyssey, Dynamotion, ropeway, and 3D space theater.

7) Park Street

Kolkata's most legendary boulevard for fine dining, live jazz music, iconic bakeries (Flurys), and sparkling festive illuminations.

8) Birla Mandir

Magnificent carved white marble temple built by the Birla family, dedicated to Lord Krishna and Radha on Ballygunge Road.

9) Birla Planetarium

One of the largest planetariums in Asia, modeled on the Buddhist Stupa at Sanchi, offering immersive astronomy shows.
